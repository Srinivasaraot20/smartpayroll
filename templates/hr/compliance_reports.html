Statutory Rules Reference

Employees' Provident Fund is a statutory requirement. Both employee and employer contribute 5% of basic salary. Due date is the 15th of the succeeding month.

ESI provides medical benefit coverage. Employee contribution is 2% and employer contribution is 3% of gross salary. Due date is the 15th of the succeeding month.

TDS is calculated on gross salary minus eligible deductions. Deposited monthly before the 7th of the succeeding month. Returns filed quarterly (Form 24Q).
